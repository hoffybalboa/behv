Privacy policy

BEHV is a personal automation tool with one user, its author. This policy describes what it does with Google account data.

What it accesses

With the account owner's consent through Google sign-in, the agent reads and modifies Gmail messages and labels, reads and writes Google Calendar events, reads and writes files in Google Drive, reads and writes Google Docs and Sheets, and reads contacts. It does not have permission to send email.

Where the data goes

The agent runs on the account owner's own computer. Data it reads from Google stays on that computer. It is not sent to any other person, company or service, is not sold, and is not used for advertising. Requests to a language model carry only the text needed to do the task at hand and are not retained by the agent.

What is stored

An OAuth token on the owner's computer, so the agent can act without signing in each time, and working files the agent produces, such as a draft or a note. Nothing is stored on a server.

Sharing

None. There are no third parties.

Revoking access

The account owner can remove the agent's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, which invalidates the stored token.

Contact

Through the support email shown on the Google sign-in screen for this app.
